How does sink water return to the toilet?

The water from washing your hands passes through filters and fills the toilet cistern. When the sensors find a problem, the Arduino starts an ozone cleaning cycle. Pick a stage or just watch.

    Parts and connections

    The physical placement of the filters is an assumption made for the drawing: separate housings outside the toilet, in series on the pipe between the sink and the cistern. It is also assumed that the ozone line joins between Valve 1 and the ceramic filter, and that backup water feeds the cistern from the top. If the real installation differs, the diagram should be updated to match.
